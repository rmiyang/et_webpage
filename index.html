01

How the model works

Evapotranspiration (ET) combines water vapor leaving the crop and exposed wet surfaces. This calculator applies a bulk Penman–Monteith energy balance separately during the light and dark periods, then adds the two.

λE = [ΔA + ρcₚ × VPD / rₐ]
÷ [Δ + γ(1 + rₛ/rₐ)]

Here A is available net energy (W/m²); Δ and γ are vapor-pressure slopes (Pa/K); VPD is in Pa; ρ is air density; cₚ is air heat capacity; rₐ is aerodynamic resistance; and rₛ is canopy resistance (s/m). Divide latent heat flux by the latent heat of vaporization and integrate over seconds to obtain L/m².

The canopy uses rₛ = leaf resistance / (active leaf fraction × LAI). Dark-period leaf resistance is multiplied by an editable factor. Exposed water uses rₛ = 0 and is calculated on its own surface area. Total liters scale with planted bed area; no universal system or cultivar multiplier is applied.

02

What to measure first

  1. Climate at canopy heightLight- and dark-period air temperature and RH, inside your actual growing space.
  2. Radiation and canopy sizeUse measured available net radiation when possible. PPFD conversion is a rough energy proxy; LAI controls the canopy resistance.
  3. Daily water balanceMeasure tanks and trays at the same point in the pump cycle. Separate drainage, leaks, and condensate returns.
No universal, validated database covering every hydroponic system × cultivar × growth stage was identified. The selectable values are starting assumptions, not fitted research coefficients.

Assumptions you can inspect

Light → available energy

PPFD ÷ 4.57 estimates PAR W/m²; this spectrum-dependent conversion is editable, especially for LEDs. For sunlight, total shortwave is approximated as PAR ÷ 0.45; for LEDs, only PAR is included. An editable 0.8 energy factor and canopy interception 1 − exp(−0.65 × LAI) give a rough canopy energy proxy. It omits net longwave and storage; dark-period proxy energy is zero. Exposed water receives 92% of transmitted light, or 92% of unshaded incident light for the reservoir. Override these proxies with measurements.

Crop & stage defaults

LAI starts at 0.3, 1.2 or 2.5 for seedling, developing or full canopy; leaf resistance starts at 200 s/m for every crop, with a 4× dark multiplier. Active leaf area starts at 0.5 × LAI, so canopy resistance is leaf resistance ÷ (active fraction × LAI). These are illustrative, editable values. Cultivar choice is recorded without changing physiology.

System & airflow defaults

Exposed wet area starts at 2% for NFT, 3% for DWC, 10% for substrate drip, 15% for ebb & flow, 1% for aeroponics and 8% for wick. Low, gentle and strong air movement set rₐ to 200, 100 and 50 s/m. These assumptions need local calibration; substrate and intermittently wet systems are approximations. Pressure starts at 101.3 kPa; adjust it for elevation.

Limits & sensitivity

The sensitivity range varies available radiation by ±20% and crop leaf resistance by ±30%, holding other inputs fixed. It is not a statistical uncertainty interval and can miss other errors. Negative vapor flux is clipped to zero; condensation is not simulated. Young crops, high CO₂, salinity, roots, and water heat storage need more detailed models. Outdoor makeup excludes rainfall entering the system.
